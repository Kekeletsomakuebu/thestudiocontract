About

Every studio needs a contract.
Every artist deserves to read it.

The Studio Contract exists because too many talented Basotho artists and producers learn the business side of music the hard way — after a deal has already gone wrong.

Most music business education isn't written for us. It assumes a legal background, a US or UK context, or a manager who already explained the basics. This publication is different: it's written plainly, for the artist recording their first single or the producer selling their first exclusive license.

We're not a blog, a law firm, or a portfolio. We're an independent publication — built to educate before exploitation happens, one guide, article and interview at a time.

Everything here is free to read and free to download. This is an independent project, founded and edited by Kekeletso Makuebu, and built one collection at a time.
