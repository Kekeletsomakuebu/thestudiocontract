\# What is LESCOSAA?



If you've been around the music industry in Lesotho, you've probably heard someone ask:



\> "Have you registered with LESCOSAA?"



For many artists, that's where the conversation ends.



But understanding \*\*what LESCOSAA actually does\*\* is far more important than simply knowing its name.



\---



\## What is LESCOSAA?



LESCOSAA (Lesotho Copyright Society of Authors and Artists) is Lesotho's collective management organisation (CMO).



Its role is to help creators manage certain copyright-related rights by licensing the public use of music, collecting eligible royalties and distributing those royalties to its members according to its rules.



LESCOSAA was established under Lesotho's copyright framework and became a provisional member of \*\*CISAC\*\* in 2024, connecting it to the world's largest international network of authors' societies.



\---



\## What does LESCOSAA do?



Some of its responsibilities include:



\- Licensing certain public uses of music.

\- Collecting eligible royalties.

\- Distributing royalties to members.

\- Promoting copyright awareness.

\- Educating creators about their rights.



Think of LESCOSAA as an organisation that helps creators manage certain rights collectively instead of every artist negotiating every public use individually.



\---



\## Who should register?



Registration may be suitable for:



\- Songwriters

\- Composers

\- Artists who write original music

\- Producers who create original compositions



Registering does \*\*not\*\* guarantee immediate income.



It simply allows LESCOSAA to identify you as a rights holder when royalties become payable under its mandate.



\---



\## Where do royalties come from?



Many people think royalties only come from Spotify or Apple Music.



That's not true.



LESCOSAA mainly deals with royalties generated from the public use of music, including:



\- Radio broadcasts

\- Television broadcasts

\- Public performances

\- Music played in businesses

\- Other licensed public uses



Streaming income may be handled through distributors or other rights-management organisations depending on how your music is released.



\---



\## Common Misconceptions



\### "Registering means I'll start getting paid."



Not necessarily.



Royalties depend on factors such as how your music is used, whether those uses are licensed and whether your works have been properly registered.



\---



\### "LESCOSAA owns my music."



No.



Your copyright remains yours unless you transfer ownership through a legal agreement.



LESCOSAA manages certain rights on behalf of its members.



\---



\### "LESCOSAA collects every type of music income."



No.



Music income can come from streaming, publishing, live performances, sync licensing, beat sales, merchandise and many other sources.



LESCOSAA is only one part of the wider music rights ecosystem.



\---



\## Why This Matters



Understanding copyright is just as important as making great music.



Knowing how royalties work helps creators protect their work, make informed business decisions and avoid common misunderstandings.



The more you understand the business behind music, the better prepared you'll be for opportunities throughout your career.



\---



\## Key Takeaway



LESCOSAA is an important part of Lesotho's music industry.



It helps creators by managing certain rights, collecting eligible royalties and promoting respect for copyright.



However, registering with LESCOSAA is only one step.



Every creator should also learn about copyright, publishing, licensing, distribution and royalties to build a sustainable music career.
